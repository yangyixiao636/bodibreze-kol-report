二、吸引效果：CTR + 3s完播率双指标解读Attraction: CTR + 3s Dual-Metric Framework

👆 CTR（点击率）= 商品点击数 ÷ 商品曝光数

反映的是曝光→点击的转化效率。观众在看到视频封面/前几帧后，是否愿意点进来？
CTR高 = 封面/开头有吸引力，观众决策"点进来看看"
CTR低 = 封面没有触发点击欲望，需要优化封面或开头帧 Measures exposure→click conversion. Does the thumbnail/first frames make viewers decide to click?
High CTR = thumbnail/opening compelling enough to trigger click decision
Low CTR = thumbnail fails to trigger click; needs thumbnail/opening optimization

TOP10 CTR区间分布：
高CTR（>5%）：@diyanaarefn 8.56%、@syuhxdx 8.06%、@parisaardina 6.33%、@syafiqahrosee 4.82%
中CTR（1-5%）：@mirasyafira_ 4.15%、@deesara 2.66%
低CTR（<1%）：@umisyamazlan 0.43%、@ooi 0.35%、@dilasaidins 0.19%、@angelynneandersen 0.21%
⏱ 3s完播率 = 3秒内继续观看 ÷ 视频总播放数

反映的是视频留住观众的能力。观众点进来后，前3秒是否让他愿意继续看？
3s高 = 开头有强吸引力，观众不想划走
3s低 = 开头无聊或封面与内容不符，观众立即离开 Measures how well the video retains viewers. After clicking, does the first 3 seconds make them keep watching?
High 3s = opening compelling; viewers don't want to scroll away
Low 3s = opening boring or misleading; viewers leave immediately

TOP10 3s完播率区间分布：
强Hook（>35%）：@diyanaarefn 56.1%、@syuhxdx 41.8%、@parisaardina 36.7%
中Hook（15-35%）：@angelynneandersen 23.6%、@deesara 22.9%、@syafiqahrosee 22.8%、@mirasyafira_ 19.1%
弱Hook（<15%）：@umisyamazlan 14.4%、@ooi 10.5%、@dilasaidins 9.9%
🔑 CTR 与 3s完播率的四种组合类型 Four Combinations of CTR + 3s Retention：
✅ 双高型：高CTR + 高3s
@diyanaarefn（8.56%/56.1%）、@syuhxdx（8.06%/41.8%）、@parisaardina（6.33%/36.7%）
最强内容类型：封面点击+内容留存双赢。Strongest type: both thumbnail and content are compelling.
⚠️ CTR高但3s低：点击驱动型

封面诱人但内容留存差——进来的人都跑了，或进来后失望离开。Click-worthy thumbnail but poor retention — viewers click but leave quickly or feel misled.
⚠️ 3s高但CTR低：留存驱动型

点进来的人都看完了，但进来的人太少——封面/开头没能在滑动中触发点击。Viewers who click watch to the end, but too few people click — thumbnail/opening fails to trigger clicks during scrolling.
❌ 双低型：低CTR + 低3s
@dilasaidins（0.19%/9.9%）、@umisyamazlan（0.43%/14.4%）、@ooi（0.35%/10.5%）
封面不吸睛，内容留不住——漏斗从顶部就塌了，需要全面重构。Uncompelling thumbnail AND poor retention — funnel collapses from the top. Complete rework needed.
